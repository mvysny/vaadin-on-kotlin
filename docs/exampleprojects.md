# Complete List Of Examples

The following is a complete list of all example projects demonstrating vok or its parts:

- [vok-helloworld-app](https://github.com/mvysny/vok-helloworld-app): VoK starter — Vaadin, Kotlin, Gradle
- [beverage-buddy-ktorm](https://github.com/mvysny/beverage-buddy-ktorm): full VoK example app on Vaadin Flow + Kotlin + ktorm — Gradle, Karibu-Testing, JUnit 6
- [bookstore-vok](https://github.com/mvysny/bookstore-vok): the Vaadin Bookstore tutorial app ported to VoK — Vaadin, Kotlin, Gradle, Karibu-Testing, JUnit 6
- [vok-security-demo](https://github.com/mvysny/vok-security-demo): VoK authentication + authorization demo — Vaadin, Kotlin, Gradle, Karibu-Testing, JUnit 6
- [vaadin-kotlin-pwa](https://github.com/mvysny/vaadin-kotlin-pwa): VoK PWA task-list demo — Vaadin, Kotlin, Gradle, Karibu-Testing, JUnit 6
- [vaadin-coroutines-demo](https://github.com/mvysny/vaadin-coroutines-demo): Kotlin coroutines + Vaadin demo — Kotlin, Gradle, Karibu-Testing, JUnit 6
- [karibu-helloworld-application](https://github.com/mvysny/karibu-helloworld-application): Karibu-DSL hello-world (no VoK) — Vaadin, Kotlin, Gradle, Karibu-Testing, JUnit 6
- [karibu-helloworld-application-maven](https://github.com/mvysny/karibu-helloworld-application-maven): Karibu-DSL hello-world, Maven variant — Vaadin, Kotlin, Maven, Karibu-Testing, JUnit 6
